Our approach

Playful, yet purposeful.

Montessori is about following the child. Our rooms are thoughtfully prepared so children can choose, explore and discover at their own pace, guided by qualified, caring practitioners.

  • Sensorial

    Hands-on materials that sharpen the senses and build the foundations for logical thinking.

  • Creative arts

    Painting, making and messy play that let imaginations run free and small hands grow strong.

  • Language

    Stories, songs and conversation every day, building a real love of words, reading and sharing.

  • Our world

    Nature, cultures and the seasons, with daily outdoor time in our garden and local walks.

  • Maths & discovery

    Counting, sorting, building and simple experiments that turn "why?" into "wow!"

  • Music & movement

    Rhythm, dance, yoga and instruments that help children express themselves and stay active.

A day with us

Rhythm, routine and room to wonder.

Every day has a gentle shape, so children feel secure, with plenty of freedom inside it to follow their interests.

Open Monday – Friday, 7:30am – 6:30pm, 51 weeks a year. 15 and 30 hours funding accepted.

  1. 7:30

    Hello & breakfast

    A warm welcome and a healthy breakfast together.

  2. 9:00

    Montessori work cycle

    Children choose activities and dive deep into what excites them.

  3. 11:00

    Garden time

    Fresh air, planting, mud kitchens and big movement.

  4. 12:00

    Lunch & rest

    Freshly cooked meals, then a quiet time to recharge.

  5. 14:00

    Creative afternoon

    Art, music, stories and group projects.

  6. 16:30

    Tea & home time

    A tasty tea and a proper catch-up with you at pick-up.
